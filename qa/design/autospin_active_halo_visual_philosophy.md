## Auto Circuit

The active autospin state should feel like a live machine circuit rather than a static toggle. The visual language is a compact neon ring: cyan motion energy, gold confirmation, and a subtle magenta inner charge that links it to the V Slot cabinet.

The halo must remain secondary to the spin button. It should sit behind the existing `АВТО` control, expanding just enough to say “automatic mode is running” without stealing attention from reels, lines, or balance. The craft lives in restrained glow, clean circular geometry, and a premium slot-machine finish.

The asset should be image-first and animation-ready: transparent background, crisp edges at small size, and enough luminous structure to survive repeated pulsing without becoming a noisy blob.
