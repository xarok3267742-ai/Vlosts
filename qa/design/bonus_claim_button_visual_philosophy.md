# Bonus Claim Button Visual Philosophy

## Treasury Claim

Bonus claim actions should feel like a harmless virtual-coin reward control, not a generic play button. The button art uses a treasury tray: violet glass, gold rails, cyan cabinet light, and a quiet center for the existing bitmap label.

The asset stays textless and image-only. It supports `label_claim_bonus` for ready/rescue states, while cooldown and OK states use calmer modal controls instead of reward styling.

The design must never imply real-money payout, cashout, withdrawal, prizes, or external value. The polish belongs to a social-casino fantasy cabinet and communicates only virtual coins.

The final button is tuned to the existing 180dp by 52dp frame, with a pressed state that feels physically depressed and does not shift layout.
