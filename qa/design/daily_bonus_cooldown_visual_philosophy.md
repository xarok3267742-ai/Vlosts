# Daily Bonus Cooldown Visual Philosophy

## Locked Radiance

The cooldown state should feel like a premium treasury mechanism resting between rewards, not an inactive error state. Its visual language is a guarded vault glow: violet glass, cyan timing arcs, restrained gold rails, and a quiet lock silhouette that communicates waiting through form and rhythm rather than text.

The composition must be meticulously crafted around the existing dialog hierarchy. Energy gathers behind the bonus badge and lower action lane, while the center stays calm so the Russian image labels remain readable. The artifact should look painstakingly tuned, with transparent edges, deliberate spacing, and no visual noise that would make the modal feel cheaper.

Material matters: the gold should feel like polished trim, the cyan like cabinet electronics, and the purple like deep casino glass. The image must avoid real-money cues, cashout suggestions, and celebratory payout bursts. This is a social-casino cooldown state for virtual coins only, expressed as locked ambience rather than reward explosion.

The final layer should look like the work of a master interface artist. Every arc, glow, particle, and rail must appear intentionally placed, compatible with Android downscaling, and strong enough to make the cooldown modal feel store-ready while remaining purely decorative and accessibility-neutral.
