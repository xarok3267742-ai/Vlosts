# Daily Bonus Reward Visual Philosophy

## Regal Spark

The daily bonus reward should feel like a premium slot-machine payout moment: a compact burst of gold, violet glass, radial energy, and coin motion that frames the chest without competing with the Russian image labels. It must look meticulously crafted, like a deliberate reward animation layer from a polished casino-style game interface.

The asset should avoid text and avoid generic confetti. The visual language is a controlled treasury glow: warm coins, purple light, cyan pinpoints, and a soft circular halo that suggests value while preserving the product promise that everything is virtual. Master-level execution means the center remains readable, the edges fade cleanly into the modal, and every sparkle feels placed rather than sprayed.

Composition should support Android scaling. The top center carries the strongest energy behind the bonus badge, the lower half stays subtler so the CTA and body image remain clear, and the full panel receives enough transparent detail to make the dialog feel alive. The result should look painstakingly tuned, not loud.
