## Lobby Radiance

The lobby cards should feel like illuminated casino doors: each game invites the player through material light, not explanatory text. The atmosphere sits above the existing card artwork as a transparent glow layer, adding premium depth while preserving the card title, reel preview, and play button as the dominant readable elements.

Violet Fortune carries a jewel-night signature: violet glass, cyan sparks, and small gold coin reflections arranged around the edges. Roman Reels carries a ceremonial gold signature: warm laurel light, bronze reflections, and controlled cyan accents that keep it connected to the rest of V Slot. Both overlays must look meticulously crafted, as if tuned to the actual card composition instead of sprayed over it.

The composition avoids clutter in the center. Light belongs in corners, edges, and behind the implied reel area, leaving titles and button labels clean. Craftsmanship is expressed through layered transparency, exact symmetry, and small repeated highlights that make the cards feel alive without becoming noisy.

The final expression is image-only and decorative. It adds visual confidence to the game selection screen, making the first interaction feel closer to a real slot lobby while keeping accessibility handled by the existing resource-backed content descriptions.
