# Low Coins Caution Stage

The low-coins modal is not a loss screen; it is a controlled recovery moment inside the arcade cabinet. The visual language should feel like a service panel lighting up when the player cannot spin: side lamps, warning rails, and a restrained rescue gauge. It must stay entertainment-only and avoid any cashout, prize, deposit, or real-money signal.

The stage layer is decorative and image-only. It sits above the generic modal panel but below the rescue glow, badge, Russian body copy, and action button. The center remains deliberately quiet so the image labels remain readable on 360dp devices. The craft is in alignment, small light details, and restrained depth rather than extra text.

The result should make the modal feel like a premium slot-cabinet state, not a flat warning dialog. Violet/cyan/gold accents connect it to the existing slot UI while keeping the action clear: either claim the daily bonus or acknowledge that the bonus is cooling down.
