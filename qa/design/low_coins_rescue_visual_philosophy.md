## Bonus Rescue

The low-coins state should feel like a casino cabinet offering a graceful recovery path, not a dead end. The visual language is a controlled rescue signal: warm coin light, violet glass, and a small pulse around the action area when a daily bonus can help.

The badge and button carry the message, so the supporting graphic must stay behind them. Light should gather around the top emblem and lower action zone while keeping the body copy calm and readable. The effect should make the modal feel useful, not punitive.

For the wait state, the same image can settle quietly as background atmosphere. For the bonus-available state, a finite pulse gives the player a clear visual cue that the button is an actionable lifeline. No text is added; the graphic communicates through material and timing.

The final image must be transparent, reusable, and decorative only. It should strengthen the slot-product feeling while preserving accessibility through existing resource-backed descriptions.
