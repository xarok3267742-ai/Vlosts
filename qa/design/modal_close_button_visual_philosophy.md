# Modal Close Button Visual Philosophy

## Cabinet Closure

Modal close actions should feel like small cabinet controls, not borrowed privacy buttons. The button art is a polished glass tray with cyan rails, restrained gold trim, and a quiet central channel for the existing bitmap label.

The asset stays textless and image-only. It supports Russian copy through `label_close`, keeps accessibility on the button itself, and avoids any cashout, prize, purchase, or real-money cue.

The form is tuned to the actual modal button frame, so Android scaling does not stretch a mismatched asset. Pressed state should feel physically depressed rather than simply darker.

The final effect should be meticulous but calm: enough depth to match the slot cabinet, enough restraint to keep result, paytable, and rules dialogs readable.
