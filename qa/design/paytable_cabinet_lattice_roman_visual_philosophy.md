# Roman Payout Lattice

The Roman payout panel should feel like a polished service drawer from a ceremonial arcade machine: bronze rails, marble-vein reflections, amber lamps, and precise turquoise measurement lines. It must remain a functional paytable surface first, so the artwork frames odds and symbols without fighting them.

The visual structure carries the theme rather than text. Laurel nodes, thin temple-like dividers, and warm glass shadows communicate Roman Reels as a distinct cabinet. Every line should look placed with painstaking attention, as if the layer was calibrated against the payout rows and bitmap digits by a master-level craftsperson.

The layer is decorative and image-only. It implies premium social-casino hardware while keeping the compliance message clear: virtual coin information only, no real-money value, no prizes. The material richness belongs to the machine fantasy, not to monetary promise.

The center must stay readable at narrow widths. Gold accents sit on row gutters and margins; row interiors stay restrained. Masterful execution means no accidental clutter over symbols, multipliers, or the close button.
