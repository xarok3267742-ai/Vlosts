## Payout Lattice

The paytable should feel like a cabinet service panel: a precise payout map mounted inside the same premium machine as the reels. The lattice gives the rows a physical grid without turning the dialog into plain documentation.

Material is violet glass, cyan inspection rails, and restrained gold anchors. The treatment must look meticulously crafted and engineered, while staying compliant: virtual coin odds only, no cash, prize, or real-world value cues.

The rhythm follows the table structure. A top cradle supports the slot title and bet digits, vertical rails align with the symbol and 3x/4x/5x columns, and a lower tray supports the footer and close action.

The layer is decorative and image-only. It improves hierarchy, depth, and readability while keeping all Russian copy in existing image labels, bitmap digits, and accessibility strings.
