## Neon Rulecraft

The footer rule strip should feel like a cabinet-engraved instruction plate, not a paragraph placed on top of art. The text remains sparse and functional, but it is treated as a luminous object: layered shadows, cool glass glow, and tightly controlled line rhythm make the rule readable while preserving the slot-machine fantasy.

The visual hierarchy is three beats: wild substitution, bonus-trigger rule, and social-casino compliance. Each beat gets its own color temperature so the player reads the mechanic quickly without scanning a block of copy. The bonus line carries the highest saturation because it is the new behavior introduced by free spins.

Craftsmanship matters more than decoration. The asset should look meticulously crafted, with deliberate stroke weight, glow radius, and line spacing. It should survive downscaling into the modal without becoming muddy, because this is a production control label, not a concept mock.

The result must stay image-first. Accessibility text carries the full explanation, but the visible UI remains a polished bitmap label that matches the rest of the cabinet language and avoids platform text widgets entirely.
