# Paytable Row Visual Philosophy

## Lacquered Voltage

The row panel should feel like a small, precisely machined part of the slot cabinet: dark lacquered glass, controlled neon edges, and restrained metallic accents. It must look meticulously crafted, not decorative for its own sake; every highlight exists to separate symbol, multiplier, and rhythm while staying quiet behind the actual payout content.

Color carries the premium mood through deep violet, ink blue, cyan glints, and small gold details. The center stays calm so symbols and bitmap digits remain dominant, while the outer rim does the visual work of making the row feel expensive. The result should read as the product of deep expertise and patient calibration.

Form is horizontal and fast, with beveled borders, thin dividers, soft corner ornaments, and faint glass reflection. The design should suggest real slot-machine hardware without copying any existing cabinet. It needs painstaking attention to balance: enough glow to feel alive, enough restraint to avoid muddying the game information.

The final asset must be almost entirely visual, with no text and no fake labels. It should look like a master-level UI artist refined the same component repeatedly until the row had depth, polish, and reliable legibility at small Android sizes.
