# Signal Lattice

The push permission prompt should feel like a guarded control panel inside the game, not a generic system upsell. Its visual language is a quiet lattice of signal lines, glass reflections, and coin-gold control points that support the bell badge without implying prizes, cash, or pressure.

The composition must be meticulously crafted around hierarchy: the badge and title remain dominant, the body remains readable, and the two action buttons remain clean. The new image layer lives behind everything, giving the modal more depth through radial energy and bottom rail structure rather than through extra words.

Color is restrained to electric cyan, violet, and small gold accents. These marks suggest daily bonus reminders and game updates as permitted notification themes, while staying clearly entertainment-only. Every glow is soft enough to feel premium, never urgent or manipulative.

The finished layer should look like part of the same slot cabinet hardware system as the rest of V Slot. It is decorative, image-only, and deliberately excluded from accessibility traversal, leaving the existing Russian copy and button descriptions as the semantic surface.
