## Jewel Cabinet

Slot symbols should feel like physical prizes seated inside a polished machine, not icons floating on a flat grid. The visual language is a cabinet of dark glass pockets: each symbol receives a measured stage, a low inner shadow, and a restrained metallic rim that makes the reel area feel assembled from crafted parts.

The form language is shallow depth rather than heavy decoration. Rounded rectangular wells, glass reflections, and subtle bevels should make every cell look meticulously crafted while preserving enough transparency for the existing frame, paylines, and win overlays to remain readable. The result must look like the product of careful arcade-cabinet finishing, not an added panel pasted over the machine.

Color stays in the established V Slot world: violet-black glass, muted gold edge highlights, and a quiet cyan reflection. The backing should support both Roman and Violet themes, so the asset avoids literal objects and uses only material, light, and depth. Craftsmanship is expressed through clean gradients, exact symmetry, and restrained sparkle placement.

The final work should be almost entirely visual. No text, no instructions, no labels: the pocket itself communicates quality through material and spacing. It must remain calm enough that the premium symbol images continue to dominate, while making the full reel window feel more like a real slot mechanism.
