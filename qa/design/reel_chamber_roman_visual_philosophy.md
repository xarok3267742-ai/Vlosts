# Roman Reel Chamber

The Roman reel chamber should read as a machined temple cabinet: marble wells, bronze dividers, amber edge lamps, and a restrained turquoise glass seal. It must make the reels feel physically mounted inside Roman Reels rather than floating over a shared generic slot grid.

The design uses three image-only layers. Cell backdrops provide individual stone/glass sockets, dividers mark the 5x3 machine geometry, and the depth mask seals the window with a warm cabinet rim. Every mark is deliberately placed with painstaking attention so the symbols stay dominant.

The material richness is purely decorative and entertainment-oriented. Gold, bronze, and marble communicate premium arcade hardware, not monetary value. The center of each cell stays readable and never competes with symbols, paylines, or bitmap digits.

The result should look calibrated on emulator screenshots: edge detail where the player expects cabinet hardware, low opacity in the reel centers, and a master-level balance between Roman identity and slot clarity on both normal and 360dp widths.
