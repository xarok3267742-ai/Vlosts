Chrome Reels

The slot window should feel like a mechanical cabinet with depth, not a flat image grid. The visual language is polished chrome, thin gold rails, cyan glass refraction, and controlled shadow. Every separator must look deliberately machined and meticulously crafted, as if it belongs to a premium slot faceplate built from layered glass and metal.

The dividers are not decoration for their own sake. They create the physical idea of five independent reels while preserving the symbol artwork as the hero. The center of each reel column stays open and readable; the rails live in the spaces between columns, adding structure and depth without clutter.

The craft is in restraint: narrow highlights, soft inner shadows, and small rivet glints aligned to the cabinet rhythm. The final asset should feel like many careful passes of polish, with master-level attention to mobile readability and no heavy stripe that would fight the payline art.

The layer must be image-only and purely decorative. It should sit above the reel symbols just enough to bind them into a real machine window, but below winning paylines, spin blur, stop flashes, and glass effects so result feedback remains dominant.
