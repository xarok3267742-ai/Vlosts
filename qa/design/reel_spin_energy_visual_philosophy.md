## Spin Current

Spin feedback should feel like the cabinet has energized the reel chamber. The motion is not a flat blur; it is a transparent current running through the 5-column machine, with light caught on glass, rails, and edges.

The material language is violet pressure, cyan velocity, and a few gold sparks. It should look meticulously tuned and mechanical, never like cash, payout, or real-world prize celebration.

The image layer must keep the symbol artwork readable. Energy lives at the column edges, outer frame, and diagonal glass streaks, while the middle of each symbol cell stays clear enough to see high-quality symbols during motion.

The layer is decorative and stateful. It appears only while reels are spinning, then disappears cleanly so paylines, win halos, and result feedback remain visually authoritative.
