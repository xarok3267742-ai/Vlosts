## Reel Vault

The reel window should feel like a deep cabinet vault: symbols are not pasted on the screen, they sit behind layered glass, shadow, and machined dividers. The visual language is quiet pressure around the edges, with the center left clear for the image symbols.

Material is expressed through transparent smoke, violet-black bevels, cyan inspection lines, and restrained gold pinlight. The layer must be meticulously crafted and premium, but it must never imply cash value or real-world reward.

Rhythm follows the 5x3 reel grid. Each column receives a soft vertical well, each row receives a low mechanical shadow, and the corners carry small glints that make the slot feel physical. The result should look like painstaking cabinet work rather than decorative fog.

The mask is image-only and decorative. It improves depth and readability while preserving all symbol art, payline feedback, and accessibility strings already present in the app.
