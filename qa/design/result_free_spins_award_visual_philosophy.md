## Bonus Flare

The bonus award badge should read like a small slot-cabinet prize lamp, not an explanatory label. It exists as a compact visual punch: a round FS medallion, bitmap-like value, and a short luminous wordmark that confirms the free-spin reward without competing with the main coin payout.

The composition must stay restrained. The dialog already has a trophy/win center, so the free-spins badge should sit as a secondary overlay with enough glow to feel celebratory and enough edge discipline to avoid clutter. The badge should look meticulously crafted, with clear hierarchy at phone scale and no dependency on platform text.

Color separates mechanics: warm gold for the award rim, cyan for slot-machine glass, and magenta-violet only as an accent tying it back to the V Slot cabinet. The result should feel like a real production asset made for a casino-style UI, with every highlight and shadow deliberately tuned.
