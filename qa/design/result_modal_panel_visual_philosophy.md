# Result Modal Panel

The result modal is the payoff moment of the slot loop, so its backing panel must not show generic utility dividers through the title, payout, or free-spins award. The shared modal panel works for settings and paytable surfaces, but its internal header seam cuts across the result composition.

The dedicated result panel keeps the same glass, violet glow, and rounded cabinet frame while smoothing the internal divider in the central content area. It remains an image asset, sits above the same modal backplate, and preserves the existing stage lattice and reward burst layers.
