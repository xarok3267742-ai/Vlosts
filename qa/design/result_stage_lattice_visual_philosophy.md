# Result Stage

The result dialog should feel like a small illuminated payout stage inside the cabinet, even when the spin is not a win. The visual layer uses polished glass, radial stage light, and small calibrated coin-gold markers to make the modal feel intentionally built rather than generic.

The composition stays beneath the result badge, bitmap amount, body image, and close button. It creates a clear visual pedestal for the outcome without implying real-money reward, cashout, or external value; the energy is theatrical, not transactional.

Color follows the slot system: restrained violet depth, cyan hardware lines, and controlled gold accents. The win-specific burst remains the louder celebration layer, while this stage gives lose, win, and bonus states a consistent premium base.

The finished asset must look meticulously crafted and image-only. It should improve perceived quality, preserve all existing Russian image copy and accessibility descriptions, and remain hidden from accessibility traversal as pure visual polish.
