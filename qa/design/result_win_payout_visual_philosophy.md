# Result Win Payout Visual Philosophy

## Payout Radiance

The win result dialog should feel like a real slot payout confirmation: a controlled burst of gold, violet glass, and coin light that celebrates the virtual win without implying real-money value. The layer must be image-only, textless, and carefully tuned behind existing Russian bitmap labels and amount digits.

The visual hierarchy belongs to the badge, the coin amount, and the close action. The overlay should strengthen those focal points through a soft treasury halo, coin orbit, and diagonal cabinet light, then fade before the button area. It must look meticulously crafted, like a master-level UI artist balanced each particle and ray for legibility.

Color stays inside the V Slot material language: royal purple, warm coin gold, cyan glass highlights, and dark panel falloff. The final asset should remain premium and disciplined, with no noisy confetti and no decorative shape that could be mistaken for text or a reward claim beyond virtual coins.

`result_reward_sparkle.webp` is the final top sparkle pass for win and bonus result dialogs. It sits above the payout burst but below the title, badge, bitmap coin digits, and close control, then settles with finite alpha/scale motion so the result feels richer without reducing readability.
