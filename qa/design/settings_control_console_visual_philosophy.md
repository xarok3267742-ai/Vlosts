# Settings Control Console Visual Philosophy

## Control Console

The settings screen should feel like the service console of the same premium slot cabinet, not a separate utility page. Its visual language is disciplined machine light: violet glass, cyan control lanes, gold safety accents, and a precise lower dock that makes compliance feel intentionally engineered.

The composition should connect the gear badge, action buttons, push status, and honest-mode panel through image-only atmosphere. Light belongs around the controls and along the lower safety rail, while the center copy remains clean and readable. Nothing should shout; it should look meticulously crafted and assembled with master-level attention to spacing.

Color and material stay inside the V Slot world. Purple depth carries the panel, cyan lines imply settings circuitry, and warm gold anchors the safety promise without implying prizes or cash value. The final artifact must appear labored over, not sprayed with generic glow.

The motion is a short calibration pulse when the screen appears. It confirms the settings console is alive, then settles into a stable state. The experience remains calm, image-first, and store-safe.
