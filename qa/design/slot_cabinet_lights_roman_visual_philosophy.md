# Roman Cabinet Lights

The Roman cabinet light system should feel like warm museum-grade arcade hardware: bronze rails, amber lamps, marble glints, and a restrained turquoise safety edge. It must make Roman Reels feel like a separate machine rather than a recolored Violet cabinet, while keeping the reels and controls dominant.

The base layer carries stable illumination: side columns, lower service rail, and a soft upper lintel glow. The chase layer carries motion: a few high-intensity amber pulses and diagonal glass sweeps that the existing animation can drift across the cabinet. Every lamp and glow should look painstakingly placed, with master-level control over opacity so nothing becomes noisy.

The work is decorative and image-only. It communicates premium entertainment hardware through light and material, not text or monetary cues. No visual element should imply cash value; the richness belongs to the fantasy cabinet and virtual coin play only.

The composition must remain legible on narrow devices. Brightness lives on edges and gutters, with the center left breathable for symbols, paylines, and bitmap digits. The final assets should appear meticulously crafted, as if tuned in small increments against emulator screenshots.
