# Slot Control Buttons Visual Philosophy

## Cabinet Instruments

The bottom controls should read as real slot-cabinet hardware: glass buttons seated in metal rings, cyan edge light, violet depth, and small gold calibration details. The icons remain symbols only, so the interface stays image-based and Russian copy continues to live in dedicated bitmap labels.

The panel behind the bet and last-win values should be a quiet instrument deck rather than a text container. Its center is deliberately restrained for bitmap digits, while the side pods and rails create the impression of a machined casino cabinet. The execution must look meticulously crafted, with crisp edges, readable depth, and no accidental real-money cues.

The paytable control is a compact utility button, not a decorative sticker. Its grid icon should be immediately readable at Android button size, visually tied to the spin deck, and clearly separate from the larger spin action.

All assets should stay transparent, accessibility-neutral where decorative, and stable at the existing XML dimensions. The polish is visual only: no new monetization language, no cashout promise, no prize implication, and no layout shift.
