# Slot Control Meter Visual Philosophy

## Meter Deck

The slot control meters should feel like physical cabinet instrumentation: polished glass, cyan edge lamps, restrained gold rails, and a quiet center that lets bitmap digits stay dominant. The visual language is engineered rather than celebratory, giving the bet and last-win panels the weight of real machine hardware without adding text.

The layer must be meticulously crafted for repeat use. It should work behind both the bet panel and the last-win panel, keeping the left and right edges visually active while leaving the label and digit corridors readable. Every highlight must feel intentionally placed, as if a senior casino-interface artist spent hours tuning the material and spacing.

Color should bridge the app's violet identity with slot-cabinet electronics: cyan light for live controls, warm gold for value rails, and deep purple glass for continuity. The image should not imply real money, cashout, prizes, or external gambling. It is decorative support for virtual coins only.

The final asset should be transparent, Android-downscale friendly, accessibility-neutral, and strong enough to make the lower controls feel store-ready. It must look like master-level craft: clean edges, no muddy overlap, no noisy center, and no accidental text-like forms.
