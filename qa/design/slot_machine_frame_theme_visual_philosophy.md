# Theme Cabinet Frames

The slot cabinet frame is the physical machine body around the reel window. It must not behave like a generic panel; it should carry the material identity of each slot while keeping the symbol field readable. The work is deliberately image-based and meticulously crafted: bevels, corner ornaments, lamps, and glass scratches are all part of the cabinet illusion.

Violet Fortune uses a jewel-cabinet language: purple glass, magenta glow, cyan edge light, gold lamp rhythm, and faceted diamond accents. The interior stays dark and calm so the reel symbols remain dominant. Ornament belongs on the edges, corners, and top crest, not across the active symbol field.

Roman Reels uses a ceremonial cabinet: aged gold, dark marble, small laurel gestures, coin-like corner medallions, and restrained cyan glass traces. Its frame should feel heavy and architectural, but never muddy. The central reel area remains deep navy so marble columns, crowns, shields, and coins stay legible.
