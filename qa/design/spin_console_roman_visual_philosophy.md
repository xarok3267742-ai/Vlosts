# Roman Control Console

Roman Reels uses the lower control area as a ceremonial console rather than a generic mobile button row. The visual language is built from enamel, gold pinstripes, cyan cabinet lamps, and dark glass rails so the player reads the spin action as part of the machine body. The craft goal is a meticulously polished casino surface: every glow is contained, every ring has a bevel, and the primary action remains readable inside a dense cabinet.

The spin button keeps the high-urgency red of a slot control, but it is no longer a flat red capsule. It carries a gold rim, a darker enamel cap, internal cyan railwork, and small Roman jewel accents. The Russian action label is rendered inside the bitmap as a visual object, with shadows and highlight passes tuned to survive downscaling on a 360dp viewport.

The deck art works as a scaffold below the controls. It should feel like an illuminated rail with paytable dock, spin cradle, and right-side balancing ornament, not a separate floating card. Purple and cyan remain secondary lights; gold is used sparingly for cabinet logic and touch targets. The result must look labored over, image-based, and physically integrated into the Roman cabinet.
