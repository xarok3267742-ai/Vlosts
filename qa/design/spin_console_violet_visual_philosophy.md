# Violet Control Console

Violet Fortune should feel like a jewel cabinet: purple glass, ruby pressure, cyan edge light, and small gold lamps held inside a polished machine rail. The lower console is not a decorative strip; it is the physical control surface of the slot. Every shape is intended to look meticulously crafted, with contained glow and precise beveling so the player reads the action area as expensive hardware.

The spin button keeps the urgent casino-red action language, but it is faceted with violet gems, gold trim, and cyan railwork. The Russian label is embedded directly into the bitmap and treated like enamel signage: bold, outlined, and built to remain legible after Android scales it down. No runtime text is allowed to carry the visible UI.

The deck uses symmetry and repeated lamps to tie paytable, spin, and decorative side pods together. Purple and magenta establish the Violet identity, while gold marks touch logic and cyan traces the glass edges. The result should support the slot fantasy without stealing attention from the reels.
