## Opening Radiance

The splash screen should feel like the machine waking up before the lobby appears. It needs a short, premium signal: controlled light around the V mark, small reel-like pulses, and enough depth that the first second feels intentional rather than empty.

The visual form is a restrained ignition sequence. A circular jewel aura sits behind the emblem, with violet, cyan, and gold light arranged like polished glass and casino metal. A compact loading rail below the logo suggests reels coming online without using words or progress copy.

The composition must stay image-only and quiet. The logo remains the hero; supporting graphics should sit behind and beneath it, never competing with the mark. Craftsmanship comes from symmetry, softened transparency, and clean spacing that looks tuned to the existing splash background.

The final result should read as a real product opening sequence: no explanations, no placeholder activity indicator, just a carefully made visual pulse that makes the app feel more finished from the first frame.
