Luminous Payout

Winning feedback should feel like a precise mechanical ceremony, not a flat highlight. The visual language is built from layered glass, polished gold, cyan voltage, and violet spill light so the player reads a payout as a physical event inside the cabinet. Every glow must look deliberately placed and meticulously crafted, with the restraint of a premium machine face rather than generic game sparkle.

The halo lives around the symbol, never instead of it. The symbol remains the hero image; the halo acts as a lens, a gold rail, and a short burst of cabinet energy that points the eye to the exact winning cells. The center stays transparent so icon quality is preserved.

The composition uses radial balance and beveled geometry: a soft outer bloom, a fine cyan energy ring, a thicker golden frame, and small corner glints. The result should suggest hours of polish in a real slot art pipeline, with master-level attention to readability at small mobile sizes.

Animation should be brief and finite. The halo can arrive with a pulse, then settle into a readable state while the win modal appears. It should support the existing payline image, not fight it, and it must remain purely decorative with accessibility handled by the symbol descriptions.
